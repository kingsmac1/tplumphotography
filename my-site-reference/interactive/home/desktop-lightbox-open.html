At TPLUM Photography, we believe every image holds a story deeper than the moment it captures. Our work blends artistic vision with genuine emotion, creating photographs that feel timeless, expressive, and real.

From intimate portraits to bold brand visuals, we tell stories with intention—using light, composition, and mood to transform simple moments into lasting impressions.

Rooted in creativity and professionalism, TPLUM Photography delivers authentic visuals that elevate every subject and bring every story to life.

Hi, I’m Jeremy.

I’m the creative lead behind Tplum Photography, based in Alberta and available worldwide. Alongside a trusted creative team, I document weddings and events for couples and clients who value authenticity, connection, and timeless storytelling.

Your wedding day is more than an event; it’s one of the most meaningful chapters of your life. Every glance, every laugh, and every quiet in-between moment deserves to be preserved with intention. Choosing the right photographer isn’t just a decision, it’s an investment in how your story will be remembered.
